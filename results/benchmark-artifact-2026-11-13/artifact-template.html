OpenAlex query benchmark: PostgreSQL, ClickHouse and Athena on five Works cohorts

An engineering notebook of what was measured: seven query families, five nested Works cohorts from 2.00m to 200.31m, the exact SQL each engine ran, requested and effective settings, every recorded repeat, resource telemetry with its measurement scope, output hashes, tuning attempts including failures, storage, cost estimates and what remains unvalidated. Setups differ between cohorts; the differences are shown next to the numbers they affect.
